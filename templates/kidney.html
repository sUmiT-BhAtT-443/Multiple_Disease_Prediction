Kidney Health Precautions
💧 Drink 2–3 liters of water daily to help kidneys flush waste
🧂 Reduce salt intake — avoid processed and salty foods
💊 Avoid excessive painkillers (ibuprofen) — damage kidneys over time
🩸 Strictly control diabetes and blood pressure — top 2 CKD causes
🥩 Reduce high-protein foods if creatinine is elevated
🔬 Get KFT/eGFR blood test annually if diabetic or hypertensive
🚭 Stop smoking — reduces blood flow to kidneys over time
