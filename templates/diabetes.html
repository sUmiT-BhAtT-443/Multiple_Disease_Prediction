Prevention & Precautions
🥗 Eat balanced meals — avoid sugar, white rice, fried & processed foods
🏃 Exercise 30 min daily — walking, cycling, or swimming works great
⚖️ Keep BMI 18.5–24.9. Even 5% weight loss cuts diabetes risk by 58%
💉 Get HbA1c blood test every 6 months if glucose is borderline
🚭 Quit smoking — doubles insulin resistance and diabetes risk
😴 Sleep 7–9 hrs/night — poor sleep directly raises blood sugar
🩺 Consult a doctor if fasting glucose stays above 100 mg/dL
